Dagger & Bone Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Overview

Dagger & Bone Publisher is a private social media management application developed exclusively for Dagger & Bone Apothecary.

The application is used internally to manage, schedule, and publish content to authorized Facebook and Instagram business accounts through Meta's official APIs.

The application is not available for public registration or third-party use.

Information We Access

The application may access:

  • Facebook Page and Instagram Business account identifiers.
  • Account authorization tokens and granted permissions.
  • Social media posts, captions, images, and publishing schedules.
  • Publishing status, engagement statistics, and analytics.

How Information Is Used

Information is used exclusively to:

  • Create and publish authorized social media content.
  • Schedule and manage posts.
  • Retrieve account and publication statistics.
  • Maintain application functionality and security.

Data Storage and Security

Application data is managed through a privately operated backend with authenticated access controls. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access.

Data Sharing

Dagger & Bone Publisher does not sell or distribute account information to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Information is transmitted to Meta's services as necessary to perform authorized publishing and analytics operations. Infrastructure providers may process information necessary to operate the application.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary for the application's operation. The account owner may request deletion of stored application data and revoke Meta permissions.

To request deletion, contact the application operator using the contact information below. Requests will be reviewed and processed, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or applicable requirements.
